🔄 Return & Refund Policy

Our Policy

Since Major Rangas products are perishable food items made fresh without preservatives, we maintain strict quality standards and have a limited return policy.

We accept returns/refunds only in the following cases:

Eligible for Return/Refund

  • Product received in damaged or broken packaging
  • Product is spoiled or inedible upon delivery
  • Wrong product delivered (different from what was ordered)
  • Product not delivered but marked as delivered
  • Order placed but not dispatched within 5 business days

Not Eligible for Return

  • Change of mind after delivery
  • Dislike of taste or flavour (all products are authentic South Indian)
  • Products that have been opened and partially consumed
  • Returns requested after 48 hours of delivery

How to Request a Return

1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery via WhatsApp: +91 95008 65686 or email: orders@majorrangas.in

2. Share your order number and a photo/video of the issue

3. Our team will review and respond within 24 hours

4. If approved, refund will be processed within 5–7 business days

❌ Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placing the order, before we begin preparation.

To cancel, WhatsApp us immediately at +91 95008 65686 with your order number.

Note: Once the order is packed and dispatched, cancellation is not possible. Please double-check your order before placing.

COD Orders

If you refuse delivery of a COD order without a valid reason, it may affect your ability to place future orders. Please contact us before refusing delivery.
