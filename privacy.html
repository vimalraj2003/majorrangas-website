1. 📋 Information We Collect

When you place an order:

  • Name, email address and phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order details (products, quantity, amount)
  • Payment method (we do NOT store card details)

When you create an account:

  • Name, email and password (password is encrypted with bcrypt)
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Order history
  • Wishlist items

Automatically collected:

  • IP address (for security and fraud prevention)
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited on majorrangas.in
✦

2. 🎯 How We Use Your Information

  • Order processing — To confirm, pack and deliver your order
  • Order updates — WhatsApp/email notifications about your order status
  • Customer support — To respond to your queries and resolve issues
  • Account management — To manage your login, addresses and wishlist
  • GST Invoice — Required for generating your tax invoice
  • Fraud prevention — To detect and prevent fraudulent orders
  • Service improvement — To understand how customers use our website
We do NOT use your data for: Spam, selling to third parties, targeted advertising, or any purpose unrelated to your order.
✦

3. 🤝 Information Sharing

We share your information only with trusted service providers necessary to fulfill your order:

  • Delivery partners (DTDC, Delhivery, India Post) — Name, phone and address only
  • Razorpay — Payment processing (they are PCI DSS compliant)
  • Resend — Email delivery service

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party.

We may disclose information if required by law, court order or government authority.

✦

4. 🔒 Data Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (SSL encrypted)
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — never stored in plain text
  • Database hosted on Railway with private network access only
  • JWT tokens used for authentication with 24-hour expiry
  • Rate limiting to prevent brute force attacks
  • Input sanitization to prevent XSS and SQL injection
Payment security: We never store your card number, CVV or banking details. All payments are processed by Razorpay who are PCI DSS Level 1 compliant.
✦

5. 🍪 Cookies

We use minimal cookies and browser local storage for:

  • Cart storage — Saving your cart items between sessions
  • Login session — Keeping you logged in (24-hour token)
  • Wishlist — Saving your wishlist locally
  • Theme preference — Remembering your display settings

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

✦

6. ✅ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Correction — Update your name, email, phone or address in your account
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability — Request your order history in a readable format
  • Opt-out — Unsubscribe from promotional messages at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at orders@majorrangas.in or WhatsApp +91 95008 65686.

✦

7. 💳 Payment Security

All online payments on majorrangas.in are processed by Razorpay, a RBI-regulated payment gateway.

  • We never see or store your card number or CVV
  • Razorpay is PCI DSS Level 1 compliant
  • All payment pages are served over HTTPS
  • UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking and Wallets supported

Cash on Delivery (COD) is also available — no payment data collected.

✦

8. 👶 Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

✦
