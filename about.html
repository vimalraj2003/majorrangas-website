Who We Are

Authentic Taste, Modern Delivery

Major Rangas (Sai Food Products) is a Chennai-based brand dedicated to bringing authentic South Indian snacks, sweets and podis to homes across India. We believe that the best food comes from traditional recipes, fresh ingredients and the love of a home kitchen.

Every product we make is crafted in small batches — no factories, no mass production, no preservatives. Just the same recipes your grandmother used, packed with care and delivered to your doorstep.

We are based in T.Nagar, Chennai — the heart of Tamil Nadu's food culture. Our products are certified by FSSAI and our brand is trademark registered under Class 30.
